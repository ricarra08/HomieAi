HomeBuyer Pro
MVP Product Brief — Version 2.0 — Broad Surface, Narrow Core
April 2026
Confidential
For internal use only
Product identity
A buyer-first homebuying workspace from serious shopping to close.
Not a marketplace. Not an agent replacement. Not "AI for real estate."
Primary user
First-time or second-time buyers — from serious shoppers getting organized to buyers under contract entering escrow. Both entry points converge into the deep escrow/closing experience.
Moment we own
Serious shopping to keys in hand. The product spans the full journey, but the moat is the 21–45 day escrow-to-close window where documents, deadlines, and finances converge.
Launch motion
B2C-first. B2B2C-ready. Free during beta. Broader surface helps acquisition — buyers can discover the product during shopping, not just after offer acceptance.
Strategy
Broad Surface, Narrow Core. The product feels complete across the journey. Shopping and Offer are light but real. Escrow, Closing, Documents, Financing, and AI Copilot are built deep.
Core promise
"From serious shopping to keys in hand — you'll always know where you are, what's due, and what everything means."
Compression: Organize the journey. Explain the deal. Keep the buyer on track. Help them avoid expensive mistakes.
Design language
Warm, stress-reducing, and guided. Apple/Airbnb-inspired clarity with a calming Wheat/Bronze/Mint palette that builds trust across a high-stakes journey.

Five core workflows
  • 1
    Track homes and build readiness
    Save homes, understand affordability, upload pre-approval. The product's front door — a light but real Shopping surface that builds context before the offer.
  • 2
    Set up the offer and transition to escrow
    Enter offer details, define contingencies, complete the offer checklist, upload pre-approval and proof of funds. When the offer is accepted, the workspace deepens into escrow automatically.
  • 3
    Ingest, organize, and understand deal documents
    Upload docs as they arrive. System classifies, extracts fields, summarizes in plain English. Agent or lender can drop files via secure link. AI explains what matters and what to look for.
  • 4
    Compare finances and track cash-to-close
    LE comparison side by side. LE vs CD variance with AI narrative. Live cash-to-close that updates as numbers change. Understand what changed, what's normal, what deserves a question.
  • 5
    Track deadlines, navigate closing, avoid mistakes
    Contingency countdown, inspection red flags, wire fraud prevention, CD review, signing prep, funding timeline. AI copilot grounded in the buyer's actual deal — not generic chat.
AI Copilot role
Non-negotiable design rule
The AI adapts depth to the buyer's phase. Light education in Shopping/Offer. Full deal-context grounding in Escrow/Closing — documents, extracted fields, deadlines, open issues. Without context, it asks rather than guesses.
In Shopping / Offer
General education mode. Answers readiness questions, explains terms, provides affordability guidance. Helps buyer understand contingencies, EMD, and what to expect. Lighter grounding.
In Escrow / Closing
Full deal-context mode. Classifies documents, extracts fields, summarizes in plain English. Compares LEs and CDs. Flags variances and red flags. Explains deadlines. Drafts questions for professionals. Cites sources.
Boundary behavior
Explanation, organization, and due-diligence support only. Never acts as lender, lawyer, or agent. When it hits a boundary, it states it, suggests the right professional, and offers what it can do instead.
Build depth taxonomy
Build Deep
Escrow dashboard, Closing dashboard, document ingestion + AI intelligence, LE comparison, LE vs CD variance, cash-to-close engine, contingency/deadline tracker, alerts + urgency, inspection/title red flags, repairs tracker, wire fraud flow, AI copilot (Escrow/Closing)
Build Light
Shopping (saved homes, affordability, pre-approval), Offer (details workspace, checklist, contingency setup), Homes view, insurance binding, collaborator upload link, glossary, funding timeline, notifications, AI copilot (Shopping/Offer)
Real product surfaces — functional and useful, deliberately limited in depth.
Thin
Post-Close (celebration, deal archive, first-30-days checklist, future value prompt)
Gives the product completeness. Minimal implementation cost.
Defer
RPA builder, offer strength analyzer, counteroffer sim, full insurance product, loan type wizard, brokerage admin, marketplace, equity tracker, refi watch, ADU planner, MLS search, e-signature, mobile app
Part of the long-term vision. Does not appear in v1 UI.
90-day success definition
01
Real buyers use the product across multiple phases — some enter at Shopping, some at Escrow. Both paths show engagement.
02
The core cluster feels painful to lose. Document explanation + LE/CD comparison + cash-to-close + deadline tracking is indispensable.
03
Users report: "I finally understood what was going on" or "I caught something I would have missed."
04
Shopping and Offer feel like real onboarding. Users who start early build context that pays off in escrow. The handoff feels seamless.
05
At least one collaborator proactively uses the upload link. This signal unlocks the B2B2C conversation.
06
Clear signal on expansion: deepen Shopping, expand Offer tools, grow B2B2C collaboration, or extend Post-Close retention.
Business model
v1 — Beta
Free. Prove product-market fit with real buyers in real transactions. Revenue is not the v1 goal — engagement and retention are.
v1.5+ — B2C
Freemium. One free deal, $29/mo for Pro. Self-serve onboarding. No partnership flow blocks the buyer experience.
v2+ — B2B2C
Agent/lender partnerships. Sponsored buyer access, co-branding, per-transaction pricing. Earned after B2C signal is clear.
