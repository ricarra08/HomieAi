HomeBuyer Pro
MVP Product Brief — Version 1.1
April 2026
Confidential
For internal use only
Product identity
A homebuyer due-diligence and transaction clarity platform.
Not a marketplace. Not an agent replacement. Not "AI for real estate."
Primary user
A buyer who just had their offer accepted and is entering escrow — first-time or second-time, with a competent agent but no personal comprehension layer for their own transaction.
Moment we own
Offer accepted through clear to close. The 21–45 days when documents are arriving, deadlines are running, and the buyer has the least visibility into what is actually happening.
Launch motion
B2C-first. B2B2C-ready. The buyer gets full value alone — no brokerage adoption required. Free during beta. Revenue model validated after product-market fit.
Collaboration model (v1)
Single-player value-first. One-way secure upload link for agent/lender — no account needed. No multi-user dashboard, no realtor tools, no brokerage admin in v1.
Core promise
"From offer accepted to keys in hand — you'll always know where you are, what's due, and what everything means."
Compression: Organize the deal. Explain the deal. Keep the buyer on track.
Design language
Premium restrained warm. Quiet confidence, not playful. The visual language should feel like a high-end financial document, not a consumer lifestyle app.

Five core workflows
  • 1
    Create the deal workspace
    Buyer enters property address, contract acceptance date, closing date, key transaction details. System generates the five-phase map, expected timeline, and document checklist immediately. Active experience begins in escrow. Offer data captured as setup input only — no offer workflows.
  • 2
    Ingest and organize deal documents
    Buyer uploads Loan Estimate, purchase contract, disclosures, inspection report, appraisal, title docs, and Closing Disclosure as they arrive. System classifies each document, extracts key fields, and places it in the correct phase automatically. Agent or lender can drop files via secure link — no account required.
  • 3
    Understand and compare the financial picture
    Compare Loan Estimates side by side. See LE vs CD variance with flagged changes. View a live cash-to-close number that updates as credits and repairs land. Understand what changed, what is normal, and what deserves a question.
  • 4
    Track deadlines, contingencies, and open risks
    Live checklist: earnest money, inspection period, appraisal and financing contingencies, disclosure windows, closing tasks. Color-coded urgency. Flags unresolved risks from appraisal gaps, inspection findings, title concerns, or lender conditions.
  • 5
    Ask deal-specific questions — get clear next steps
    AI Copilot explains documents, answers questions in plain English, summarizes current deal status, and generates smart questions to ask the lender, escrow officer, or agent. Grounded in the buyer's actual transaction — not generic chat. Without deal context, it asks for it rather than guessing.
AI Copilot role
Non-negotiable design rule
The AI must always know the deal context before it answers. It is grounded in uploaded documents, extracted fields, current phase, timeline, open issues, chosen loan scenario, and pending deadlines. Without context, it does not answer — it asks for it.
In document ingestion
Classifies the document. Extracts important fields. Summarizes in plain English. Highlights sections that matter. Identifies questions, inconsistencies, or missing items. Links explanations back to source pages with citations.
In financial workflows
Explains each LE and CD line item. Compares lender offers in plain language. Flags unusual variances. Recalculates cash-to-close when numbers change. Separates facts, estimates, and interpretations clearly.
In deadline and risk tracking
Translates dates into an understandable timeline. Warns when deadlines are approaching. Explains why each deadline matters and what missing it would mean in plain terms.
Boundary behavior
Explanation, organization, and due-diligence support only. Never acts as lender, lawyer, or agent. Never provides legal or financial advice. When it hits a boundary, it states it, suggests the right professional, and offers what it can do instead.
Feature classification
Must — v1
Deal workspace, five-phase progress map, document ingestion + AI classification + explanation, LE comparison table, LE vs CD variance review, cash-to-close engine, contingency countdown + deadline tracker, alerts + urgency states, inspection red-flag summary, title/escrow red-flag summary, repairs/credits tracker, wire fraud warning flow, secure collaborator upload link
Identity
Phase stepper, persistent phase visibility, contextual glossary, alerts panel, phase transition CTAs, funding/recording timeline
What makes the product feel like a guided system, not a tool. Technically simple — emotionally critical.
v1.5
Loan type helper wizard, insurance view (quote comparison + coverage education), offer workflows, collaborator dashboard, glossary depth, notifications (email/SMS/push)
Insurance binding (binder upload + status tracking) ships thin in v1 within the Escrow dashboard. Full Insurance View is v1.5.
Deferred
Post-close phase, RPA builder, offer strength analyzer, counteroffer simulator, homes view, MLS import, brokerage admin, equity tracker, refi rate watch, ADU planner, property tax exemptions, marketplace/referral features, e-signature, mobile native app
Real and valuable — earn the right to build them. RPA and offer tools require legal review before any version ships.
90-day success definition
01
Real buyers under contract upload real documents and return multiple times during escrow — not just testers clicking around.
02
At least one workflow cluster feels painful to lose. Likely: document explanation + LE/CD comparison + cash-to-close clarity.
03
Users report: "I finally understood what was going on" or "I caught something I would have missed."
04
At least one realtor proactively sends the upload link to a buyer without being asked. This signal unlocks the B2B2C conversation.
05
Clear evidence for the next expansion direction: backward into offer prep, outward into B2B2C, or forward into post-close retention.
06
Core workflows show consistent repeat usage: document uploads, deadline checks, AI questions tied to live deal state — not one-time novelty use.
Business model
v1 — Beta
Free. Prove product-market fit with real buyers in real transactions. Revenue is not the v1 goal — engagement and retention are.
v1.5+ — B2C
Freemium. One free deal, $29/mo for Pro. Self-serve onboarding. No partnership flow blocks the buyer experience.
v2+ — B2B2C
Agent/lender partnerships. Sponsored buyer access, co-branding, per-transaction pricing. Earned after B2C signal is clear.
