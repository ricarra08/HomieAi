# Arizona Real Estate Transaction Workflow

## 1. Transaction Timeline (Acceptance to Close)

**Typical Timeline:** 30-35 days (financed) | 14-21 days (cash)

| Day | Milestone | Notes |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| 0 | Contract acceptance | AAR Residential Resale Purchase Contract executed |
| 0-3 | EMD deposit | 3 business days to deposit with title/escrow company |
| 1-10 | Inspection period | 10 calendar days; silence = acceptance (buyer waives) |
| 5-10 | BINSR filed (if any) | Buyer's Inspection Notice and Seller's Response |
| 5 biz days | Title review | 5 business days after receipt of commitment |
| 5 cal days | HOA doc review | 5 calendar days from receipt |
| 7-14 | Appraisal ordered/completed | Embedded in loan contingency |
| 21-30 | Financing contingency | Separate addendum (AAR Loan Contingency Addendum) |
| 25-30 | Clear to close | Final underwriting approval |
| 28-33 | Sign closing docs | Parties sign separately (escrow closing) |
| 30-35 | Fund and record | 1-3 business day gap between signing and recording |

**Day Counting:** Calendar days. If deadline falls on weekend/holiday, extends to next business day.

## 2. Contract Types and Key Fields to Extract

### AAR Residential Resale Purchase Contract
The single dominant form in Arizona. Key fields:

- **Parties:** Buyer name(s), seller name(s), marital status
- **Property:** Address, legal description, APN
- **Price:** Purchase price, loan amount, down payment
- **EMD:** Amount, deposit deadline, escrow holder
- **Financing:** Loan type, loan amount, interest rate cap (if any)
- **Close of Escrow (COE):** Target date
- **Possession:** At COE or other arrangement
- **Fixtures/Personal Property:** Included/excluded items
- **Warranties:** Home warranty election
- **HOA:** Name, fees, transfer fees
- **Contingency dates:** Inspection period, financing deadline
- **Addenda:** List of attached addenda

### AAR BINSR (Buyer's Inspection Notice and Seller's Response)
Critical inspection negotiation form:

- **Inspection items:** List of requested repairs or credits
- **Buyer election:** Approve as-is, request repairs, or cancel
- **Seller response:** Agree, counter, or refuse (triggering buyer's right to cancel)
- **Deadlines:** Response periods

### AAR Loan Contingency Addendum
Not automatically included — must be separately attached:

- **Loan type and amount**
- **Contingency deadline (21-30 days)**
- **Appraisal contingency** (embedded within)
- **Loan application deadline**

### AAR Seller's Property Disclosure Statement (SPDS)
- Property condition details
- Known defects
- Environmental hazards
- Insurance claims history

## 3. Contingency Mechanics and Deadlines

### Inspection Contingency
- **Duration:** 10 calendar days from acceptance
- **Mechanism:** Buyer must file BINSR to negotiate or cancel
- **Critical Rule:** Silence = acceptance. If buyer does not file BINSR within 10 days, buyer is deemed to have accepted the property's condition
- **BINSR Process:**
  1. Buyer submits BINSR with disapproved items
  2. Seller responds (agree, counter, or refuse)
  3. If seller refuses, buyer may cancel within 5 days of seller's response

### Financing Contingency
- **Duration:** 21-30 days (per Loan Contingency Addendum)
- **Mechanism:** Separate addendum required — not part of base contract
- **Appraisal:** Embedded within loan contingency
- **Failure to obtain financing:** Buyer may cancel and recover EMD

### Title Contingency
- **Duration:** 5 business days after receipt of title commitment
- **Mechanism:** Buyer reviews title commitment for exceptions/defects

### HOA Document Review
- **Duration:** 5 calendar days from receipt
- **Mechanism:** Buyer reviews CC&Rs, financials, rules

### General Contingency Framework
- Arizona uses a **passive contingency removal** model
- Silence = acceptance (contingencies expire, not actively removed)
- No active contingency removal form required
- EMD cure period: 3 business days if deposit deadline missed

## 4. Required Inspections and Disclosures

### Common Inspections
- **General home inspection** — standard whole-house
- **Flat roof inspection** — critical in AZ due to UV/heat degradation
- **Pool inspection** — very common (Arizona has highest pool density in US)
- **Scorpion/pest assessment** — common in desert regions
- **Well water quality testing** — arsenic and nitrates testing (rural/unincorporated areas)
- **Termite/wood-destroying organism (WDO)** — standard
- **Sewer scope** — especially older homes
- **HVAC inspection** — critical given extreme heat conditions

### Required Disclosures
- **AAR SPDS (Seller's Property Disclosure Statement):** Standard form, common law duty to disclose (Hill v. Jones)
- **Affidavit of Disclosure:** Required for properties in **unincorporated areas** of the county
- **Lead-based paint disclosure:** Federal requirement for pre-1978 homes
- **HOA disclosures:** CC&Rs, financials, meeting minutes

## 5. Insurance Requirements

### Homeowner's Insurance
- **Policy type:** HO-3 (standard)
- **Average cost:** $800-$2,500/year
- **Major concerns:** No significant wind, earthquake, or wildfire concerns in most metro areas
- **Required by:** Lender (for financed purchases)
- **Timing:** Binder required before closing

### Title Insurance
- **Market rate pricing**
- **Custom:** Seller customarily pays owner's policy; buyer pays lender's policy
- **Provider:** Title/escrow company

### Homestead Protection
- **Type:** Asset protection only ($400,000 cap)
- **Tax exemption:** NONE — Arizona homestead does not provide property tax reduction

## 6. Tax and Fee Summary

### Transfer Tax
- **NONE** — Arizona does not impose a real estate transfer tax
- **Affidavit of Value:** $2 filing fee with the county recorder

### Mortgage Tax
- **NONE**

### Property Tax
- **Rate:** ~0.7-1.2% of assessed value
- **Fiscal year:** July 1 - June 30
- **Payment:** Two installments (Oct 1 / Mar 1)
- **Limited Property Value:** Capped at 5% annual increase (assessment cap)
- **Proration:** Prorated at closing based on fiscal year

### Closing Cost Estimates
- **Title insurance:** Varies by purchase price
- **Escrow fees:** Split buyer/seller (customary)
- **Recording fees:** Minimal
- **No transfer tax, no mortgage tax**

### Non-Resident Withholding
- **State:** 2% of gross sales price (ARS §43-1072)
- **Federal:** FIRPTA applies to foreign sellers

## 7. Closing Process Specifics

### Escrow Closing Model
Arizona uses an **escrow closing** process through a title/escrow company:

1. **Escrow opened** at contract acceptance
2. **Title search and commitment** issued
3. **Parties sign separately** — buyer and seller do not need to attend same signing
4. **Lender docs received** by escrow
5. **Signing** — buyer and seller execute closing documents
6. **Funding** — lender wires funds to escrow
7. **Recording** — deed recorded with county recorder
8. **Disbursement** — escrow disburses funds to seller

### Key Closing Details
- **Sign → Fund → Record:** 1-3 business day gap between signing and recording
- **No simultaneous close:** There is typically a gap between signing and recording
- **RON (Remote Online Notarization):** Limited/evolving — check current status
- **Community property state:** Both spouses must sign deed of trust, regardless of title vesting
- **Escrow holder:** Title/escrow company (not attorney)
