# Realtor Demo Feedback & Follow-Up

## Insights & Suggestions Received

### Workflow & Compliance

- **State-specific workflows** — The entire workflow should adapt based on the state in which the home is being purchased (disclosure requirements, timelines, legal forms, etc.)
- **State-specific inspection requirements** — Every state has separate inspection requirements; the appraisal summary needs to reflect these differences
- **HOA compliance** — Add HOA compliance tracking (document collection, rule review, fee schedules)
- **Condo docs** — Include condo document collection and review as part of the workflow

### Financial Tools

- **DTI (Debt-to-Income)** — Include DTI calculation and visibility throughout the process
- **Insurance section** — Add a dedicated insurance section (homeowner's, title, flood, etc.)
- **Cash-to-close estimator** — Integrate a cash-to-close estimator starting from the offer phase and refining through closing
- **EMD receipts** — Add earnest money deposit receipt tracking and confirmation
- **Post-close principal calculator** — In the post-close phase, provide a principal calculator so users can see how additional payments reduce the lifetime of their mortgage

### AI & Shopping Phase

- **Aggressively build out the shopping phase** — This phase needs significantly more depth and functionality
- **AI-powered shopping customization** — Integrate AI so users can define and refine their wants/needs, then watch matching under-contract properties in real time

## Follow-Up Questions for Realtors

### State & Regulatory Variance

1. Which 3–5 states do you most commonly work in, and what are the biggest workflow differences between them that trip up buyers?
2. Are there states where the inspection/appraisal process is so different that it would need an entirely separate flow, or can most be handled with conditional steps?
3. How do you currently keep track of state-specific disclosure and compliance deadlines — is that purely manual today?

### Financial Visibility & Estimators

1. At what point in the process do buyers most often get surprised by their cash-to-close number? Where would an early estimate have the most impact?
2. How granular should the DTI display be — just a single ratio, or a full breakdown of debts vs. income with "what-if" scenarios?
3. For the post-close principal calculator, what additional payment increments are most realistic for your typical buyers ($50/mo, $100/mo, lump sums)?
4. Do your buyers typically track EMD on their own, or is that something you're fielding questions about constantly?

### Insurance & HOA

1. What insurance types are buyers most confused about or most likely to overlook (title, flood, PMI, umbrella)?
2. For HOA/condo deals, what documents are the biggest bottleneck — and who usually handles collecting them (buyer, agent, title company)?
3. How often do HOA compliance issues actually delay or kill a deal?

### Shopping Phase & AI

1. When a buyer says "I want X," how often do those criteria change after the first few showings — and what triggers the change?
2. Would it be more valuable for the AI to proactively surface properties that *challenge* the buyer's assumptions (e.g., a neighborhood they hadn't considered) or strictly match their stated criteria?
3. What data points about a property matter most to you as the agent that buyers don't typically think to ask about?
4. How much of the shopping phase is collaborative with the agent vs. the buyer browsing independently — and where does the handoff happen?

### Prioritization

1. If you could only have three of these features ready for your next closing, which three would save you the most time or prevent the most client confusion?
2. What is the single most painful manual step in your current process that you'd want automated first?
3. Are there any features we showed today that you think would actually *slow down* your workflow or create more questions than they answer?

### General

1. What did we *not* show today that you expected to see?
2. Who else on your team (transaction coordinator, lender, title officer) would need to interact with the app — and what would their ideal view look like?
3. Would you be open to a follow-up session where we walk through a real past deal and map it against the app's flow?
